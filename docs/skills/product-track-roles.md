# Product Track Review Roles

Roles that weigh in on **product-track** decisions (what the project is for, who it serves, positioning):

| Role | Scope |
|------|-------|
| Content Strategist | Profile messaging, audience alignment, positioning, brand voice |
| Technical Lead | Technical credibility, project selection, architecture claims accuracy |
| Business Development | Engagement channel strategy, consulting availability, lead qualification |

## Track assignment rationale

- Content Strategist: explicitly about mission, audience, positioning → product
- Technical Lead: portfolio projects demonstrate what the project is for (capabilities) → product
- Business Development: engagement types define who the project serves → product

## Statement

Four engineering philosophy principles declared in profile:

1. **Agent-Driven Development** — AI agents as first-class team members, not autocomplete. Durable context > prompt engineering.
2. **Knowledge as Infrastructure** — Decisions, facts, and guardrails are versioned, reviewable, and enforced — same as code.
3. **Pragmatic Craft** — Ship iteratively. Measure what matters. Optimize for change. Technical excellence serves product outcomes.
4. **Systems Thinking** — Local optimizations fail. Model the whole: data flow, team topology, incentive structures, failure modes.

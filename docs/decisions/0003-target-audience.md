## Motivation

Optimize profile for technical decision-makers who hire senior/staff engineers and technical advisors, not for peer engineers or general audience.

## Context

The profile emphasizes architecture decisions, team leadership, business impact metrics (20+ microservices, 2-3x performance), and consulting availability — all signals relevant to hiring managers and technical leaders.

## Tradeoffs

- Pro: Directly addresses who makes hiring/engagement decisions
- Pro: Justifies including business metrics and leadership experience
- Con: May feel less relatable to individual contributors
- Con: Technical depth must still be credible to peer reviewers

## Decision

Write for technical leadership audience. Include metrics, architecture scope, leadership scope. Keep technical depth high but frame in business/team impact terms.

## Motivation

Position as a senior engineer building LLM-powered applications (RAG, agents, eval) and backend/platform systems, explicitly excluding model training/ML research.

## Context

The three key projects demonstrate this blend: virage (RAG pipeline), kms (agent knowledge management), edap_model (scientific computing). The resume shows .NET/Cloud, GenAI integration, and platform engineering.

## Tradeoffs

- Pro: Clear differentiation from ML researchers and pure backend engineers
- Pro: Aligns with market demand for "AI Engineers" who build LLM applications
- Con: May exclude some pure backend roles that don't value AI skills
- Con: "AI Engineering" term is still evolving in industry

## Decision

Lead with "AI Engineering · Backend/Platform · Agent-Driven Development" in headline. Feature virage and kms as primary AI engineering showcases; edap_model as technical depth demonstration.

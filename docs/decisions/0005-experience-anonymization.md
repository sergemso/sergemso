## Motivation

Keep focus on technical achievements and scope without tying to specific employers, supporting B2B/contractor positioning.

## Context

The profile represents an independent contractor available for engagements. Company names are on LinkedIn/resume; the GitHub profile should emphasize what was built and the technical scope.

## Tradeoffs

- Pro: Cleaner, more portable across engagements
- Pro: Aligns with contractor/consultant branding
- Pro: Avoids potential conflicts with current/past employers
- Con: Less verifiable without cross-referencing LinkedIn
- Con: Loses brand recognition from known companies

## Decision

Show role titles, timeframes, tech stacks, and quantified achievements only. No company names. Link to LinkedIn for verification.
